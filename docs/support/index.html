Keen Kids support

For help with any Keen Kids app or game, email our team at support@keen.kids.

Report a problem

Include the app or game name, your device, and the app version. Tell us what you tried and what happened. A parent or guardian can contact us on a child’s behalf. Please leave out your parent PIN, your child’s name, and private browsing history.

Keen Browser help

Approve a website

When Keen holds a new site in Strict mode, choose Ask a parent and hand the device to a parent. The parent can review the address and approve the page or the site. To change an approval later, open Parent settings.

Clear browsing history

Open History in the browser menu. Swipe a visit to delete it, or use the trash button to clear visits and recently closed pages. To clear the separate record of approvals and blocks, open Parent settings → Family activity → Clear activity.

Ask about privacy

Read the privacy policy for details about on-device storage, websites your family visits, and how to request deletion.
