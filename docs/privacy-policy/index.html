Privacy policy

Updated November 22, 2026

This policy covers Keen Browser 1.0 for iPhone and iPad, this website, and email sent to Keen support.

What stays on the device

Your family can use Keen Browser without a Keen account. It saves the school group, filters, approvals, bookmarks, and browsing activity on the device. These records let Keen apply your choices, reopen pages, and show parents what was allowed or blocked. If you set a parent PIN, Keen protects its verification data in the device’s Keychain.

When a page needs a model check, Keen uses an available on-device Apple model or a model a parent has downloaded. The check processes page text, such as a headline or a video caption, on the device. Downloading an optional model connects to Hugging Face and its file delivery service, which receive the model request and your IP address.

Who receives browsing requests

Opening a website sends requests to its publisher and to services that supply its content. They can receive your IP address, browser information, the requested page, and information you enter in a form. Searching the web sends your search terms to the selected search engine, Brave or Bing.

Using YouTube in Keen sends requests to YouTube and Google for listings, searches, thumbnails, video details, captions, and playback. Those services can receive search terms, video or channel identifiers, and network information even when browsing signed out. Each publisher sets its own retention periods for the information it receives. Google’s privacy policy explains its practices.

What parents can remove

Keen keeps up to 300 visits, 20 recently closed pages, and 300 family activity events on the device. Older entries roll off as new ones are added. In Parent settings → Bookmarks and history, you can remove saved pages and clear visits. In Family activity, choose Clear activity to remove recorded decisions and their video details.

You can revoke approvals in Parent settings. Reset family settings clears the school group, filters, and preferences. The PIN, approvals, and history have separate controls. Clearing a device record affects that device only. For information already sent to an outside website, use that provider’s privacy controls.

This website and support email

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising, or sign-up forms. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub’s privacy statement describes its handling of visitor data.

If you contact support, Keen and its email provider receive your email address, message, and any attachments so the team can respond. Please send only the details needed to help with your question.

Contact Keen about your family’s data

A parent or guardian can ask about information shared with Keen or request deletion of a support message. Email support@keen.kids from the address used to contact us so we can locate the conversation. Please leave your child’s identifying details out of the request.
